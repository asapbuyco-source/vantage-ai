5) PREDICTION QUALITY / ROI FIX  (BACKEND)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- EV-FIRST pick selection (was 50% probability, which favoured short-odds
  favourites → negative ROI).                                     [quant_pipeline.py]
- Safety-downgrade GUARD: the pipeline no longer replaces an +EV pick with a
  short-odds "safe" market (DC 1X, Over 1.5, Under 3.5) unless the safe market
  stays +EV and within 3pp of the original EV. Otherwise it KEEPS the EV pick.
- Templates replace slow per-match AI analysis (instant, bilingual EN/FR,
  thousands of combinations, no API/rate limits).  [backend/analysisTemplates.js
  + backend/quantService.js]
