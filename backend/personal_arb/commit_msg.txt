fix(shot+verify): refuse wrong-market highlights + warn when a line is not on the page

Diagnosed with the Brentford-Chelsea and Connah's Quay events:
- the alert math is correct (0.74% = worst case at exactly 3 goals; 1.46% is the
  naive figure that ignores the quarter-line split)
- but betfrenzy's feed O/U line (1_3, e.g. 3.0,3.5 / 2.5,3.0) is NOT rendered on
  their page for many events (verified: 8 retries over ~40s, no split row; only
  'Over/Under 2.5 Goals' and 'Total Goals' rows 0.5/1.5/3.5...). The old picker
  then highlighted a WRONG section ('Full Time & Over/Under 2.5'), producing a
  screenshot that did not verify the alert.

Picker fixes:
- lineOf now parses split titles to their mid ('Over/Under 2.0,2.5 Goals' -> 2.25)
  and signed AH pairs to their mid ('-1.0,-1.5' -> -1.25); AH distance by magnitude
- tolerance: quarter lines require an EXACT match (0.05), half/integer 0.35 —
  a section further away is a different market and is refused
- when refused, a separate Telegram warning is sent ('could NOT locate this bet...
  treat this leg as UNVERIFIED') plus the screenshot caption carries the warning
Verified: Connah's now refused ('closest off by 0.25'); Brentford has no split row.
